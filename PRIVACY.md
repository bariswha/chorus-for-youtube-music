# Privacy

Chorus has no backend, no analytics and no tracking. The authors never receive any of your data.

## What stays on your device

- Your keys, friend list, messages and settings, in `chrome.storage.local`.
- Your listening stats: which songs you played, for how long, and when. They power the Stats page and your yearly recap. You can turn recording off, export your stats or delete them in **Settings → Stats**.

## What other people can see

| Who | What |
| --- | --- |
| People in your Jam | The display name you chose in Chorus, the Jam queue, chat messages and reactions. |
| Your friends | Whether you're online, the song you're playing (you can turn this off in Settings), whether you're in a Jam, and your stats summary if you turn on **Let friends see my stats**. |
| Public relay servers | Only encrypted data, plus your IP address and connection times, like any server you connect to. |
| LRCLIB | The title, artist and duration of a song when you open its lyrics. |

## What Chorus does with YouTube Music

Chorus reads the song you're playing from the page you have open and controls the player in that tab: it opens songs, seeks, pauses and adjusts the playback speed slightly to stay in sync. To show song details and make suggestions, it asks YouTube Music's public `next` endpoint without sending your cookies.

Chorus never reads your Google account, never sees your password and never acts on your account. It doesn't skip or block ads. Songs that YouTube Music marks as private or as your own uploads are never shared with anyone.

## Deleting your data

To remove everything, uninstall the extension. That clears all local storage. You can delete your stats alone in **Settings → Stats → Clear stats**.
